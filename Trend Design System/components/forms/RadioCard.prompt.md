Selectable card with a radio — payment method (COD pre-selected), delivery option, address pick.

```jsx
<RadioCard name="pay" value="cod" checked icon="banknote" title="الدفع نقداً عند الاستلام" description="ادفع نقداً لمندوب التوصيل عند استلام طلبك." />
```

Selected = `--surface-tinted` + `--border-brand`. Options that don't exist yet (ShamCash, Paymera) are **hidden by flag, not greyed**.
