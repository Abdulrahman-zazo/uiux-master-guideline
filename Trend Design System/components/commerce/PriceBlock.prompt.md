Every price in Trend renders through this — tabular figures, currency at 62% size, discount styling built in.

```jsx
<PriceBlock amount={249} />
<PriceBlock amount={174} compareAt={249} size="xl" />
<PriceBlock amount={89} currency="AED" size="sm" />
```

A discounted price is the **only** place body-level text is allowed to be purple.
