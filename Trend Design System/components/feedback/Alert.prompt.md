In-page message — 16px-radius tinted panel with a leading glyph. No border, no shadow.

```jsx
<Alert tone="success" title="Order placed">We've emailed your receipt.</Alert>
<Alert tone="danger" title="Payment declined" action={<Button size="sm" variant="secondary">Try another card</Button>} />
```

`info` uses the brand purple tint — the only tinted panel that carries brand colour.
